You are a lamp,
and you hum all alight
with a sound so distinct
and so grand, above all,
droning into the night
with your chord, low and tall.

</image>

The factories glow
on the Delaware's banks
and reflect off the tide,
but beneath your burnt light
there is nowhere to hide.

<image>
src="lamp1.jpg"
</image>

All your friends and dear colleagues
are impoverished to compete,
like a bent-backed bridge tollway
spanning Lewes's breadth
up to good old Cape May.
