10/12/26

The sun melted--
and I do mean it *melted*--
like a fat blob of ice cream
on a fresh-baked peach cobbler.

Its yolk zigged and zagged
between cloud, sky, and truss,
setting gracefully, with ease,
beneath a steel-braced bridge.

I stood on the footbridge
that once commanded trains,
and I saw just at once
the Midwest's bleak protest,

and I thought of those folks,
so cruelly enslaved,
and the heart-breaking curse
that once rode this broad river.

I was a dog wearing goggles
racing against highway headwinds,
my soft tears drying quick
as my eyes stung with sunlight.

I don't believe in God,
but if I did,
I'd think that this
was one of his finest works.
