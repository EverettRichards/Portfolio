#stormy
10/13/26

Move on.

There is no candid sound
you could brazenly squeeze
from your candied red lips
that would change my firm mind
and make me come back.

Move on.

You say that you're sorry,
but you don't seem to see
that the sin you remember
is opaque to me;
why carry it forth?

Move on.

No, it wasn't this reason,
nor that, nor another,
that turned me away
from your love on that day,
and bode me not to stay.

Move on.

Every word that I scribble
seems to cut you so deep,
so I just cannot see
why you still want to think
that you're in love with me.

Move on.

So I won't bother you
any more than I have,
and I'll let you go fester
with whomever you choose:
it's your love to lose.

Move on.
