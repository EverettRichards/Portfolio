#stormy
8/30/26

"Thus always to tyrants",
as the old saying goes,
and said Lincoln's assassin
as he climbed over rows

of shot-startled patrons
who squinted in awe
at the semi-star brother
that before them, they saw.

A tyrant is ruthless
of course, but please see
the compassionate strife
of complete monarchy;

for in her bold tempo
there is love and deep care,
though I wouldn't cast blame
if you're still unaware.

Do bid me, and listen:
her power depends
on your tired belief
that she's tied all your ends,

and the frame of advice
is ill-suited, at best,
and you know it's an order,
not a humble request.

So I urge you, dear reader,
to raise your right hand
and shake it with vigor
'til they all understand

that you're not the meek damsel
you've been led to believe,
but in fact, a great warrior
far too grand for their sieve.
