#stormy
9/3/26

When I pause and reflect
on my recent thoughts
and the poems I've written,
I can't help but notice

that for these past weeks,
I've only been inspired
by one thing alone,
and that one thing is you.

So I thought to myself
that I might move on easier
if I could write a poem
that wasn't about you,

only to realize
that in making it my purpose
to distract myself
from my feelings for you,

I have only confirmed
your stubborn residence
in the time-misted catacombs
of my burgeoning mind.

...

The Delaware moon settles high
in this rainy and grey-clouded sky,
and I can't help but ask myself why
this ever had to be goodbye?
