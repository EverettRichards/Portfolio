9/21/26

I stumble on through
the old wooden door
of the klondike-dive bathroom
where I make my night's vice,

and I palm-wipe the glass
to see the tourmaline rage
in my shit-faced reflection,
inundated by muddy tides.

We live in a digital age
so contradictory,
where there are lies,
but no more secrets;

once knowledge is spoken,
it will forever be,
like prize-fighter Aurum
in an intrepid ravine,

and of course, only found
by the villains you flee,
who wait with their feathers
and tar for your skin;

And yet, when you sift
the coarse dirt through your fingers,
you'll be fucked-short by Pyrite
and assayed nothing more,

and the cold truth you long for
is buried 'neath mountains
of bulletproof paywalls
and checkered esteem.

So tell me, dear stranger,
in this bitch-ridden bar,
what have I to lose
if I dance 'round in jest,

and what truth do you think
that I may find tonight
if I put down my glass
and go slumber in peace?
