9/16/26

They say sober thoughts
are at risk to become
drunk words, though my mind
is still not quite numb;

And you think you see meaning
'tween the lines in my po'ms,
like burden-free whispers
in their ramshackle homes;

And I wouldn't quite blame you
to misread my aim,
but to go love another
is not part of my game;

You can sift through my words,
but that's not the issue;
see, I'm not drunk enough
to tell you that I miss you.
