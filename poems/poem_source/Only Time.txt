#stormy
9/1/26

* Written on the airplane heading back to San Diego at around 3AM central time.

It stings to know I've walked astray
to chase beautiful lies,
for I never thought I'd be betrayed
by her pale blue stormy eyes;

and so I see, despite my strife
to fight these thoughts unjust,
that only time can mend
the canyon-ridges in my trust.

And so I lie here, half-awake
in bed, with curtains drawn,
while darkness floods my screen-lit room
from dusk to lonely dawn;

and as I reach for bourbon trace,
I learn well from the devil
that only time can lower
my blood alcohol level.

So while I sit and sober up
and drift to dreamy places,
I just can't help but call to mind
some long-forgotten faces;

at once, I see that my past self
wouldn't guess the life I've led,
and that only time can tell me
what strange journeys lie ahead.
