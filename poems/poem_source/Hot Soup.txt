8/19/26

* A poem inspired by one particularly hot bowl of leftover soup.

I lift my spoon
and sip some broth
but havoc does unfold

when lightning crumbs
slip through my gums
and my poor tongue I scold.

I try at once
to tame my meal
and blow with all my might,

but at this rate,
I estimate
that I'll be here all night.

But then, I figure
if I try
to localize my war,

I'll end the plight
of "not quite right"
and serve a little more.

But even so,
I harshly find,
so much to my disdain,

that in my bowl
is not my goal,
but boiled beef and pain.

So here I sit
with stomach growls
and notches on my tongue,

but know, sublime
I've got much time
to learn while I am young.

And never will your
efforts be surveyed
by giant men,

yet still, there's hope
-- don't weep and mope! --
of pigs to fill your pen.

So if you've tried
and all your efforts
throw you for a loop,

remember, blowing
on your spoon won't
cool your bowl of soup.
