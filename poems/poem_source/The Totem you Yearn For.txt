9/25/26

Like Orwell, you know
you don't want to be loved
so much as to be
endearingly understood,

and you see through the rhymes
that befuddle my prose
that the thing you call love
is the loneliest drug.

For to go love another
is to cut yourself off
from the world's bright offers
and its signal-rich winds,

and you feel, after years,
that your brilliant crusade
is now nothing more
than a daily commute.

So aim your sights inward
to reckon with being,
and scatter your focus
wherever you please,

but know, though you try
to break free from your worries
that the totem you yearn for
is a bleak prison cell.

Keep your friends close,
and keep your own conscience closer.
