I hear childish voices taunt me:
"You're in love! You're in love!"
And at once, I can feel
the endearment they offer
as they validate my truth
that I am, indeed, in love.

And though I see through
the superstitions of my youth,
which whispered to me
in a sinister tone
that "the one" was nearby,
narrowly evading my gaze,

and in spite of my insistence
against fate and free will,
I still choose to believe,
in my heart of hearts,
that we are meant to be,
whatever that might mean.

Still I know,
despite the pale heartache
that darkens my day,
that at the end of my life
I'm promised a great peace
eternal, irrevocable, and absolute,

and yet I prefer
to persist and feel pain,
this strange, sick reminder
that I'm here and I can
feel the current's inescapable pull
and smell the breeze's sweet perfume.
