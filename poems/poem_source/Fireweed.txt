10/02/26

They say that a rumor
can spread like wildfire,
and of course, they mean
quickly, crudely, indiscriminantly,
propagating in all directions,

but what they don't tell you
is that the scorched trees
often remain upright,
coated in their own charcoal
but alive nonetheless,

and that, way up north,
the fireweed races
to fill the nurtured soil
and bring magenta vibrance
to the forsaken land,

though not quite as fast
as the fires that first
had to rip through the forest
to make room for new life.

To the faces and foes
I had ne'er before seen,
I was worse than a stranger:
I was well known;

And to the friends I have found
a mere two seasons later,
I am born again,
and though the soil is still
rich and battered with soot,

all they can see is the fireweed.
