10/02/26

By the time I come back to San Diego,
another season will have passed.

The soft, verdant leaves
that I ride by every day
will have faded and fallen
in damp piles by the street,

The winds that I race against
on my northward commute
will only hasten their flights
and grow cold as they grow restless,

The day-one acquaintances
I bump into here and there
will become a core part
of my weekly routine,

The troubles that burden
my heart and my mind
will be soft-superseded
by new strifes and worries,

And the memories of home
that I idly hold
will be less pure than ever
as my new life unfolds.

Another season has passed;
that's how I got to Delaware.
