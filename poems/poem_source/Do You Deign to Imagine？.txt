11/9/26

* Main idea: The search for objective truth is a futile and fundamentally misguided venture. Accept and embrace what you don't--and never will--know.

The remarkable thing
about verdant truth
is that it persists
despite all disbelief,

and yet, one can never
be more sure than not
that the world one conjures
exists past sensation.

But is that the goal?
To render the bounds
of our region encapsuled?
I'll humbly protest:

In a world so devoid of meaning,
we are at liberty to invent it
wherever we design;
do you deign to imagine?
