Very pastoral. I think it's telling the story of a world in which the Earth stopped rotating / the day cycle ended?

Seven lanterns wander down a brittle meadow,
scattering their hollow light
while the solemn moon lingers
behind a crooked thimble,
peeking through
like a pebble on the third layer beneath the surface.

Meanwhile, velvet voices tumble
in wistful tides across the orchard,
crumbling in the distance
with no compass to guide them onward,
the dusky sky fluttering far behind them
beside that restless yellow marble in the sky.

Storied stones gleam and glint beneath the cobblestones
that bear the weight of fickle feet as they roam
and stray so far from their homely hearths,
unraveling their days besides bushes and brambles
that drowsily beckon with their quarries of berries.

A rugged creek murmurs across the grove,
its gentle gurgle drifting through the fleeting days,
and its waters awaiting the amber kettle concoctions
that rattle on hot stoves,
giving way to pillars of steam
that sway wearily with the breeze.

Seven coatless bodies shiver
before a tender-floored chapel
where the daylight once spilled through the windows,
back in the ancient times,
when the Earth still clambered about its faint axis
and bountiful harvests won ribons of praise;

But now, the poor sparrows freeze in fractured nests,
for the golden spring sun no longer hastens to rise,
leaving behind a wayward celestial cellar
with one side so desperately turning thicket to kindling
and the other, but a hellscape still trembling with dead echos.
