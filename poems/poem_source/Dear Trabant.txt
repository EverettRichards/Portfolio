* A short poem about the Trabant Center at the University of Delaware. I'm sure something goes on there. But, to me, it's just lunch and a restroom break.

Dear Trabant, to me
you are little more than
a Chick-Fil-A counter
and a place for a piss,

but forgive me--I'm sure
some grand things do air
between your brick walls,
and within your grand halls,

though I don't really care
to stick around here
to discover whatever
you might have in store.
