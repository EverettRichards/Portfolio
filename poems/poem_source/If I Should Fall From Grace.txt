10/02/26

If I should fall from grace,
you should know that my efforts
were all spent in vain
chasing bone-breaking pavement
over blood pedigree.

If I should fall from grace,
you ought to leave me behind
like the leather-bound wallet
that didn't say "hello"
on your way out the door.

If I should fall from grace,
please be warned by my devotion
and my failure, in loving just one,
while my furrow-browed friends
stood crinkled in contempt.

If I should fall from grace,
I urge you not to mistake
my plain feelings for manifestos;
for the only coherency found here
is by coindidence alone.

If I should fall from grace,
remember always that freedom
is the fundamental virtue:
from it, all others can be derived;
without it, others arrive only by chance.

If I should fall from grace,
let me fall, and fall, and fall,
and do not try to arrest me
with a blanket and pillow;
to fall, thus, is my destiny.
