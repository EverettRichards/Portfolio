#stormy
10/25/26

O.K., fine, I'll admit
that you've caught me off guard,

and you're working towards something
that's honestly hard

and in spite of the troubles
that, at home, have you buried,

there's something about this
that just makes me worried.

The Northeast's chilly winds
will blow more than you'd think,

and you can't always trust
what comes out of the sink;

so just pray that the Hudson's
gray odor lends mercy, 'cause

what the fuck do you think
that you'll find in New Jersey?

...

Well, I certaintly can't stop you--
I'll bet nobody can.

I just don't want you tied up
in some stranger's van!

I've burned bridges, sure,
but I care for your safety:

I've unblocked your number.
If you need something, call me.
