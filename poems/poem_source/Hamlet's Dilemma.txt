8/18/2026

In my life, I have known too many people
who have contemplated suicide --
Friends, family, and strangers alike.

When asked about their reasons
(or when their wits are volunteered),
I often hear the same few things:

"Life is meaningless."
"I have no purpose."
"The world is cruel, unjust, and painful."
"What's the point?"

But when I ask people what they think
the meaning of life is,
no two answers are alike:

"To love,"
"To lose,"
"To teach,"
"To learn,"
"To serve God,"
"To seek purpose,"
"To win and to lose,"
"To laugh and to cry,"
"To forgive and forget,"
"To serve your community,"
"To be served by your peers,"
"To see everything the world has to offer,"
"To leave the world better than you found it,"
"To enjoy all the world's offerings, brilliant and mundane,"
"To give thanks to your parents for the life they've given you,"
"To give life to children of your own someday,"
"To find happiness in everyday life,"
"To bring happiness to others,"
"To praise and to scorn,"
"To uplift and uphold,"
"To kick back and relax,"
"To celebrate,"
"To notice,"
"To wonder,"
"To mourn,"
"To be."

And at first, this unsettles me.

In many matters I am familiar with,
there is one single right answer
and there are many wrong ones;

For all the trivia hosts who ask,
"What is the capital of Indonesia?"
There is only ever one Jakarta.

And so, I feel saddened
to think that maybe,
death and nonexistence *are* the solution
to the great unanswerable question of life,
and that purpose is only found in delusion
in a desperate evolutionary attempt
to justify our continued existence.

But then I realize
that true, transcendent meaning
is not bound by the arbitrary correlations
we find in our anthropogenic logic,
but rather, it is free
to be whatever one wishes
and that all the reasons to exist,
and to keep going, are *valid* reasons
as long as they encourage you
to keep existing
and to keep searching
for fulfillment in your own way.

The cup is not half empty,
nor is it half full.
We're mincing words.
The cup merely is,
and the water merely is.
Lift the cup.
Drink the water.
Live.
