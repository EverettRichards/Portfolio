9/05/26

Was I ever really ready for the journey?
I once thought I was,
but that was before I knew
what the journey would entail;
and I still don't know.

Can you ever be ready
for something so unknown?
At first, I found it hard to leave,
but after breaking free, I found
that I don't really want to go home.

And still, I know that
the journey has only just begun,
and my startled heartbeat
scampers 'tween my ribs,
afraid of what's to come.

...

There'll be someday when my eyes
no longer drift across the sky,
and my gaze no longer scatters
like newly-hatched spiders
across the beckoning clouds.

But 'til then, I'll allow
this bushy-tailed wonder
and oblivion to win,
because I might ne'er be ready
for this guideless journey,

and in hindsight, I'll know
that despite all the costs
that I've yet to encounter,
that my time here was worthwhile
and that I made the right choice.
