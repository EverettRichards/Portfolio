11/04/26

I don't think I've seen you smile today.

Did I do something wrong?
Did I say something rude?
Was my demeanor inelegant
and my manners all crude?

Or, perhaps, something happened
at the place where you work--
Did you fumble a deadline?
Was your boss a big jerk?

Or, maybe, I'm reading
some false clues in your eyes,
for I just cannot see
what's beyond your disguise.

But, alas, you just shrug
and say it's quite alright,
so I forfeit my probe
and I bid you goodnight.
