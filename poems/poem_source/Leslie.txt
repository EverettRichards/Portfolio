I know the call you sent out
was intended for me;
and I heard it.

Don't tell me I was disgusted,
that I "faked everything",
that you're undesirable,
that I somehow made you feel
like you had to be smaller --

as if that was the problem.
Not the lies, not the distance,
not the parental regime,
but somehow, your weight,
or the size of your jeans.

Do you think I'm that shallow?
That I'd want you to starve?
If I had it my way,
you'd indulge as you wish
and be full, ripe, and happy
at the end of each day.

But it's not up to me --
nor those many men you court.
The choices you make now
are yours, and yours only.

If you've got something to prove,
go prove it,
but don't say you did it for me.
I know that's not true.

Be safe with your J,
but get me out of your heart.
I want no role in this play:
it's a game meant for two,
and I'm no longer one.

At the risk of self-reference,
I bid you,

Move on.
