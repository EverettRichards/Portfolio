#stormy
8/31/26

Last month, I held you
and kept you protected
from cool July breezes
that nipped at our limbs

and now I find myself, burning
'neath a late-August spotlight
with your hand, a few feet
from my own concealed wish

which I offer, with pity
and thirst in my eyes,
but you gently shake
your gorgeous head

as if to tell me you can't
bear the risk of withstanding
the misplaced resentment
you fear from your parents.

We were apart at once
by mere miles and mountains,
surmountable canyons
and flatlands alike,

but are now so plane-close,
like the East and West Germans,
and yet so heart-far
like from London to Moscow.

And again, I can see
that you sit so close to me
and yet, there between us
is a great big divide

like that wall in Berlin
which stood tall and stretched far
keeping East from the West
and dear friends on different sides.

And I know, as I leave
that my purpose was met,
though the result was not quite
what I'd ever expect,

that by seeing you, dear one,
it only assured me
that the villains I'd loathed
were ne'er to blame

'cause your heart elected
to let them prevail
and to let them deprive you
of your overdue freedom.

And maybe, someday
that stern wall will fall
and again I'll embrace
your neighborly love,

but from here, all I see
are the pale concrete bricks
and the somber lies snaking
like rusty barbed-wire,

and the whitewater currents
that thrash through tough rocks
and bend and meander
where my eyes can't yet see.

So I'll return East
to my springboard life
and bid thanks to the West
for revealing the truth,

and I'll sleep well tonight,
for the first time in weeks,
because my confusion
has finally collapsed,

and I'll savor the sunsets
o'er Delaware skies
with fine-textured clouds
and boundless suspense,

recalling, but not longing for
the clear-skied conclusions
that contoured the breast
of the desert southwest.
