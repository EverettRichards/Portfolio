#stormy
9/02/26

My Coors Light told me to tell you
that your beauty intimidates me.
When I see your bright face,
I feel scared,
afraid that I will never again
see something so beautiful,
and that I'll roam these damp streets
for the rest of my life,
wandering and wondering
when I'll ever cross paths
with a gentle heart
with eyes like yours.

And so when I hear you say
that it's best we don't keep in touch,
I know that.
I've known that for months.
Every fiber of being I've ever ignored
has told me so.
But I couldn't admit it,
I couldn't lend air to those words,
I couldn't make my case,
and I couldn't stand my ground.
But you did.
That makes one of us
noble, courageous, and strong.
And it's you.

Are you happy with the man you have found?
Because I found him, too,
not long ago,
around the same time you did, I think.
And I think I am happy,
and I have few regrets,
but I do wish that things
could have turned out some other way.

There's plenty of girls
in class and in the halls,
and I'm sure they must be cute.
But, forgive me
if my words do seem corny,
but only you, baby,
can make me feel horny!
I only have eyes for you,
so let me run across your world;
let me run wild!

At this point,
I've circled around
the Taco Bell drive through
more times than I've circled
around this relationship,
swerving jaggedly in and out
and in and out of love,
with my plastic fin dipping above the water,
and I keep telling myself
that this time,
things will be different.
But they never are!
And yet I never regret it.
Because, how else would I know?

I wrote you a letter
that I meant to give you,
but while I traveled
across the continent
to deliver it to your hands,
the words lost their meaning,
and the card became void.
I planned to burn it
here, in my backyard,
with a turqoise candle-lighter
my parents bought on my birthday
to light my surprise candles,
and to watch its tacky carbon
diffuse into the atmosphere.
But I stopped myself short
when I re-read the letter,
baffled at how quickly
my words ran awry,
and I hoped that they'd someday
be true once again.

The beer has subpoenaed
these crude words
from my aching breast;
and my sobering mind
has chosen to cast them
into the void.

I hope you catch them.
