* This one is pretty straightforward. Consider it an ode -- a love letter, if you will -- to my new favorite vice.

It's another long day
turning thoughts into worries
as I pound coffee cups
and my swift heartrate hurries;

so tonight, I've decided
that my nerves will get lucky
with the rich, amber spoils
of central Kentucky,

which ail in a bottle
so shiny and sleek,
boasting high on a shelf
where my visitors peek;

so all of your Coors
and Coronas can miss me
'cause tonight's chosen vice
is my peanut butter whiskey.
