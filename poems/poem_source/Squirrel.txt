* Inspired by a cute little squirrel I saw rummaging around in the leaves and acorns outside Smith Hall at UD :)

Oh! To be a squirrel,
so squeamish and disquiet,

darting aimfully through
Autumn's first fallen leaves,

with an oak-nut in her mouth
and a circus in her heart.

December, she knows,
burrows just 'round the bend,

with the quick-changing wind
a brisk harbinger of winter,

and so, she prepares
with her instinctive will.

She's perched on my fence,
and I think I should feed her,

but then I think handouts
are not what she needs;

in fact, she needs nothing,
for to be is to succeed.

In the infinite chaos,
life has still found a way

to spread its deep roots
and resist Earth's disease,

the incurable infection
of the anthropocene.

So, be like a squirrel--
be carefree and wild!--

and follow the beacons
that beckon you forth,

and don't you forget
to stay fed while you bury

your bountiful harvest
for seasons untold.
