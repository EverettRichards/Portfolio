11/5/26

* Halloweekend shenanigans.

"Who are you, kind stranger?"
I asked with a grin,
while my leather-lined wrists
soiled sticky with gin.

"Can't you see?" she replied.
"I'm the wonderland's sprite",
and I humbly agreed:
"You've got wonder, alright."

So I closed out her tab
and I opened the door,
and we slid past drunk dancers
on that beer-battered floor.

She offered me booze,
and I could not resist--
"Is this one shot or four?!?"
I yelped and slammed my fist.

Then we stumbled away
to a neighborhood dive
where the two-o'-clock wind
was just coming alive,

and she pulled me aside
where her friends couldn't see,
and she showed me the craze
she'd been saving for me.

Caught off guard, I blinked
and I glanced at the door,
but she boldly leaned in
to indulge me once more.

Then she whispered to me,
and some thoughts she confessed,
but in confidence, true,
so I won't tell the rest.
