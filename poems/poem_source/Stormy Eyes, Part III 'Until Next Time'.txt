Stormy Eyes, Part III: "Until Next Time", July 31, 2026

At last, here we stand
on a warm July driveway
while teary-eyed stars
blink woefully above us,

sharing the strain we feel
as the cord that connects our hearts
is stretched once again,
and they wait for it to relax;

But instead, a steel bird
will pull that cord thin
as I fly to, and land in,
my newfangled home,

and leave us quite desperate
to relive the moments
we recently shared
on a couch, bed, or stair.

The few photos we've taken
will surely remind me
of yester-noon 'ventures
'tween holiday nights,

and I do think your voice
on my handheld machine
will soothe my ripe nerves
like it did by the Bay;

But it n'ere will compare
to the warmth of your hand
and the soft angel kisses
you leave on my lips.

I am so, so, so grateful
that our life-paths have crossed,
and I hope that someday
they'll criss-cross again,

but 'til then, my dear love,
live your life oh-so-full,
and, like me, please do savor
those bright dreams in your soul.
