11/07/26

Dear Boston, I love you,
and I'm not 'fraid to say
that we'd share every sunrise
if I had it my way,

for the way your cold breeze
feels so warm on my face
and my ails disappear
without temper or trace.

I beckon thee, humbly,
to embrace and enfold
my cold, wretched body
in your beauty untold,

and to let us forget
about past sins and vice,
and to cherish the dawn
as it melts the moon's ice.
