9/15/26

I wear my black Adidas shorts
and shirt of violent green,
and rifle through a candy bowl--
but it's not Halloween;

and soon I see, this party
isn't quite what I had dreamed,
for seven beers and everclears
in my solo cup teemed.

Inside my pocket, fingers clasp
around my sleek car key,
and for a sec I think about
the hot-and-cold AC;

but anecdotal antidotes
persuade me not to drive,
while black-and-yellow angels
beckon me, "ARRIVE ALIVE."

My black and silver bicycle
has dirt between its treads,
and flimsy coin-flip front-brake pads
today decide on "heads";

but I can tame a drunkard's bike
in my own dreams alone,
a risk not worth the stakes to lose
to a misplaced traffic cone.

...

Hold your transistor radio
and tune into my hertz;
I'll broadcast you my melodies
and show you where it hurts.
