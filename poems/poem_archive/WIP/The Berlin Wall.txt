#stormy
8/31/26

Apart at once
by mere miles and mountains
but now so plane-close
and yet so heart-far

For I know that you sit
a mere two feet before me,
and yet, a vast chasm
keeps us so far apart

like that wall in Berlin
which stood tall and so wide
keeping East from the West
and dear friends on different sides

And I know, as I leave
that my purpose was met,
though the result was not quite
what I'd ever expect

That by seeing you, dear one,
it only assured me
that the villains I'd loathed
were ne'er to blame

'cause you made the choice
to let them prevail
and to let them deprive you
of your overdue freedom

And so I'll go back
to my springboard life
and bid thanks to the West
for revealing the truth

And I'll sleep easy tonight,
for the first time in weeks,
because I've got no one
to blame for my strain

and I'll savor the sunsets
o'er Delaware skies
with fine-textured clouds,
recalling, but not longing for
the clear-skied rolling-hill sunsets
of the desert southwest.
